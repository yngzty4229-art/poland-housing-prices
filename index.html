Poland housing prices from official notarial deeds

What apartments really sell for in Poland's largest cities: the median price per m² from notarial deeds in the official Real Estate Price Register (Rejestr Cen Nieruchomości, RCN), compared with developers' asking prices from their mandatory daily price lists. Updated every week – last update 2026-10-19.

In the 12 months to September 2026 the median apartment in Warsaw sold for 15,742 PLN per m², in Czestochowa for 7,243 PLN – according to notarial deeds, not listings.

Data for a specific address

The same official sources for any address in Poland, as ready-made APIs (also usable without code, with Excel/CSV export, and from AI assistants through MCP):

  • Apartment valuation (AVM) – an estimated value for any apartment from comparable deeds
  • Transaction prices from notarial deeds – median, quartiles, trend and the list of deeds around any address
  • New-build prices – every developer offer around an address
  • Asking vs transaction price gap – both sources compared for any location

Methodology

  • Deeds: apartment sales from the RCN register (GUGiK bulk export + live web service), market sales of full ownership only, implausible prices and outliers removed.
  • 12-month window ending at the latest deed published for each city – counties publish with different delays.
  • Year on year: median of the last 12 months vs the 12 months before.
  • Asking prices: available apartments in developers' official daily price lists (dane.gov.pl), whole city.
